## 2. Key Architectural Discoveries & Evidence Synthesis

### 2.1 Orientation & Campus Placement
- When approaching and standing outside St. Paul's Main entrance facing the facade:
  - **Main Block is on the right**.
  - **Annexure Block is on the left**.
  - The ground-level gap between the two blocks is physically impassable.
- The prominent exterior sign reads `"ST. PAUL'S BLOCK"`. Annexure block has **no exterior sign**.

### 2.2 Bridge Geometry & Rules
1. **First-Floor Bridge:**
   - Connects Main Block at Room 418 / FC(3) to Annexure Block at Room 469 / FC(14).
   - Uncovered, open-sided walkway with concrete deck and metal perimeter safety railings.
2. **Second-Floor Bridge:**
   - Connects Main Block at Room 434 / FC(6) to Annexure Block at Room 474 / FC(15).
   - Uncovered, open-sided walkway with concrete deck and metal perimeter safety railings.
3. **No Ground Bridge:**
   - Ground level has no connecting bridge. Direct transit is strictly via the 1st and 2nd floor bridges.
